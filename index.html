    How to play

    • WASD or arrows walk, drive, ride
    • Shift run
    • Space jump, press again in the air to flip
    • E stamp, go inside, get in, ride, sit
    • H hold hands
    • K hop on the scooters, again to hop off
    • 1 to 0 dances (more on the dance button)
    • Tab switch Roji and Homama
    • Two phones, top right play together on two devices
    • C J wardrobe, journey
    • F full screen
    • Mouse wheel, + -, or pinch zoom the map
    • Click or tap the ground walk there
    • Thumbstick walk; push to the edge to run
    • Tap the ground walk there
    • A button stamp, go inside, get in, ride, sit
    • Up arrow jump; tap again in the air to flip
    • Two kids hold hands
    • Scooter ride around town faster
    • Music note dances
    • Face, top right switch Roji and Homama
    • Two phones play together on two devices
    • Pinch zoom the map
    • X, top right leave any game
    Turn your phone sideways Roji World plays in landscape.
