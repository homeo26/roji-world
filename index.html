    How to play

    • WASD or arrows walk, drive, ride
    • Shift run
    • Space jump, press again in the air to flip
    • E stamp, go inside, get in, ride, sit
    • H hold hands
    • 1 to 6 wave, dance and more
    • Tab switch Roji and Homama
    • C J wardrobe, journey
    • F full screen
    • Mouse wheel, + -, or pinch zoom the map
    • Click or tap the ground walk there
    Turn your phone sideways Roji World plays in landscape.
